# US-08 — Current Traceability Plan design

**Status:** owner-approved on 2026-10-02; design only, not implementation evidence
**Date:** 2026-10-02
**Scope:** PLN-001–008 and PLN-010 (P0); PLN-009 reminders remain P1

This design supersedes the unapproved 2026-09-03 US-08 draft. Read it with `docs/us/mvp-contract.md`, `docs/us/development-clarifications.md`, `docs/us/limitations.md`, and the US-08 rows of `docs/us/requirements.md`. It does not authorize a push, deployment, or release.

## Decisions and boundary

The owner confirmed on 2026-10-02 that the plan is a US-only versioned service, with an editable draft, a server-captured configuration snapshot, human-confirmed procedures, atomic approval of an immutable PDF, retained history, and a visibly distinct synthetic demo. The office UI uses the existing US EN/ES cabinet, not RU pages. The U.S. deployment remains isolated; its persistent production data and infrastructure must not be in the Russian Federation.

FDA describes the plan's required subject matter, not a mandated PDF layout: recordkeeping procedures including record format and location; how FTL foods are identified; how TLCs are assigned where applicable; a point of contact; and a farm map **if** the entity grows or raises applicable FTL food (except eggs). Plans must be updated when needed to reflect current practice, and previous plans retained for two years after update. The [FDA processor example](https://www.fda.gov/media/188100/download?attachment=) is illustrative, not a substitute for facts about a particular organization. Product copy must not call the plan FDA-approved or claim legal compliance.

P0 supports the selected processor operation that does not grow or raise FTL food. A processor profile alone does **not** prove the farm-map condition false. Approval requires a recorded assertion about this operation; a positive or unknown answer blocks P0 approval with an explicit unsupported-scope finding, rather than generating a false “not applicable” statement. Supporting farm maps and other CTEs are later scope.

## Current repository baseline

- `apps/api/src/deployment/us-development.module.ts` explicitly allowlists US routes. Controllers use `UsSessionGuard`, and store transactions reload membership and US capabilities. US-08 follows these paths; it does not register a controller in the RU module or reuse RU tenant route guards merely because the old draft names them.
- The current US browser lives in `apps/admin/src/us/`, with in-cabinet navigation and EN/ES copy. The old `apps/admin/src/pages/traceability/plans/` and RU i18n paths are not the target.
- The persisted profile has an immutable code, baseline version, IANA timezone and `retention_years` (default five, minimum two). `traceabilityRetention` is a pure calendar policy, not a storage or backup enforcement mechanism.
- TLC-source locations and reviewed product coverage records exist. No persisted global product-review cadence or verified operational backup configuration exists. These cannot be inferred from `docs/architecture.md` or a count of products.
- The US runtime currently has no plan service or US artifact-storage dependency. Existing billing PDF and object-storage code may inform a bounded adapter, but US-08 must not share a RU production bucket, credential, job or release path. Local synthetic tests may use an isolated disposable store.
- The local owner-provisioning command marks its reserved organization as synthetic. Only a trusted server-created, exactly verified seed identity may enable demo treatment; a client checkbox, tenant name, slug alone, or request body must not.

## Plan data and lifecycle

Add a tenant-scoped `traceability_plan_versions` table and migration. Each version has a stable ID and per-tenant positive number, status `draft | effective | superseded`, draft revision, schema version, sections, change summary, creator, approver and approval time. At most one draft and one effective version exist per tenant, enforced by partial unique indexes. Every lookup and lineage reference is tenant-scoped with composite keys where applicable. No plan exists for `US_GENERIC_LOT_TRACEABILITY` or `RU_CHZ`.

Draft sections are typed plain text, never HTML: record-maintenance procedures, FTL identification procedure, TLC assignment procedure, point of contact, farm-activity declaration, and review/update procedure. The record-maintenance section distinguishes system-of-record, record formats and locations, responsible roles, retention policy, and backup/recovery statements. It stores what a named operator confirms; the app does not turn a configured S3 URL or architecture prose into a claim that backups exist or restores have worked. Contact name, title and phone are required; email is optional. A real tenant requires an actor/time-stamped confirmation of operational procedures, contact and non-farm scope at approval. An assertion is an attributed claim, **not independent verification** or compliance certification.

The synthetic demo tenant may use fictional contact and procedures. Its row, plan list/detail, preview and approved PDF carry persistent `Synthetic demo — not an operational record` provenance. This is server-derived and frozen in the approved version; it is never a user-editable toggle. A real tenant cannot inherit demo treatment by copying narrative text or a version.

At approval the server freezes a typed `config_snapshot` and digest: profile code/baseline/timezone/retention, tenant display name, current TLC-source location descriptions, the actual FTL classification workflow characteristics supported by code, and the operator-confirmed procedures/contact/farm declaration. A global review cadence is narrative until it has a real configured source; do not invent a numeric cadence or claim automatic quarterly review. The snapshot records provenance for every fact (`configured`, `operator_confirmed`, or `synthetic_fixture`). Historical PDF bytes and snapshot never change when master data changes. The list/detail compares current facts with the effective snapshot and shows changed sections, without rewriting history. Changes in individual product profiles are not hidden behind an aggregate count when the document actually refers to those profiles.

Draft editing uses `expectedRevision`; an outdated save or approval conflicts. The author may approve their own draft in P0, with the approver identity and time recorded. Approval requires: processor profile, complete required sections/contact, a TLC-source location, explicit non-farm assertion, current configuration, allowed wording, real-tenant operational confirmation or trusted demo provenance, and storage readiness. A later version requires a nonempty change summary. Validation issues identify section and code. The client may display them early, but the server is authoritative.

The first approval makes v1 effective. Approving v2 atomically supersedes v1 and leaves v1's snapshot, PDF key/hash and approver intact. Effective/superseded versions have no update/delete route. Abandoned **drafts only** may be discarded with audit. A one-time approval idempotency key is bound to tenant, version and request digest; identical retry returns the same result, changed reuse conflicts. Version numbering and lineage are serialized by a tenant-scoped lock.

On supersession, store the prior version's inclusive `retain_through` civil date using the existing `traceabilityRetention` rule with the supersession date in the frozen tenant timezone, current configured years, the two-year floor and any persisted longer floor/hold. An effective version has no expiry date. P0 has no purge, cascade deletion, or generic tenant-delete path that can erase required plan history. The UI may describe the policy, but tests of the calculator alone do not prove database/object/backup retention or a restore drill.

## PDF publication and storage

The API renders English PDF bytes from one frozen model using bundled fonts and stored timestamps; no wall-clock “generated at”, network assets, live lookups or locale-dependent re-rendering. EN/ES interface selection never changes an approved PDF. The PDF includes version, effective date and timezone, approver, source/confirmation provenance, required procedures, contact, the supported non-farm statement, change history and allowed-wording limitations. The synthetic demo marker appears on **every page**. Draft preview is watermarked `DRAFT — not effective` and is not stored as a published version. PDF output is bounded and byte-deterministic for the same model and renderer version; SHA-256, byte size and renderer version are saved.

Use an explicitly configured private **US** artifact store. The adapter supports verified put, bounded read or short-lived tenant-authorized download, and deletion of only an unreferenced object created by the losing attempt. A failed upload/verification leaves the draft intact. Publication uses a unique attempt key: freeze draft/config/approver/time, render and verify outside a long DB transaction, then lock and recheck draft revision and configuration digest in a short transaction before switching statuses and storing the winning object key/hash. Concurrent approval, draft edit, or configuration change must yield one winner; never overwrite or delete a winning PDF. Orphan cleanup is narrowly scoped and auditable. Production US startup/readiness remains unavailable until a separate non-RF storage configuration is verified; local synthetic completion does not claim deployment acceptance.

## API, authorization and UI

US-only routes under `/traceability/plans`: list, create draft, read version, update draft, validate/preview draft, approve, discard draft, and download published PDF. Controller registration is explicit in the US module. Every method is documented in OpenAPI and rejects method/path aliases at the established US boundary. List/detail require `traceability.read`; preview/download require `traceability.export.read`; mutations and approval require `traceability.qa.manage`, all reloaded from current membership in a tenant transaction. Wrong-tenant IDs return 404; unsupported profile returns a safe unavailable response; RU edition has no route. All mutation, rejection and download audit records assert actor, tenant, action, target, outcome and relevant version/hash metadata.

The Plan item appears only for an authorized processor profile in the existing US cabinet. Build the versions list, section editor, validation/confirmation dialog and read-only approved detail with `@markiro/ui` tokens/components and EN/ES keys. Derived facts are labelled and linked to their source workspace; operator statements are visibly distinct. The list shows one effective version, older versions, any draft, change-impact banner, retention note and synthetic provenance. The approved detail shows PDF hash and bounded download. No P1 annual/email reminder is implemented merely because the earlier brief draws its future state. Screens must remain keyboard-accessible and usable in light/dark and desktop/mobile layouts.

## Checks and acceptance boundary

Build in independently reviewable stages: (1) domain/contracts and negative validation; (2) additive schema, retention fields and tenant/audit invariants; (3) US server configuration reader and draft commands; (4) deterministic PDF plus isolated artifact adapter and atomic approval; (5) US HTTP boundary; (6) US cabinet; (7) integrated synthetic flow and independent review. Each stage starts with a focused failing test, passes package gates, and is reviewed before the next. No push, release, real-data import or deployment is included by this design.

Required negative tests include: real tenant lacking confirmed procedures; client-forged demo marker; positive/unknown farm activity; absent TLC-source location; stale draft/config; duplicate/concurrent approvals; storage failure; PDF immutability after location/product/contact edits; cross-tenant IDs; generic/RU profile denial; unauthorized role; exact audit metadata; retained superseded PDF; no removal through application cleanup. Test a demo PDF's visible marker, deterministic bytes and English artifact invariance under ES UI. Browser inspection and real non-RF storage/backup verification remain separate evidence; do not mark them complete from unit or integration tests.

## Explicitly deferred

Farm maps, grower/aquaculture support, regulatory determination of applicability, review reminders (PLN-009), e-signature, DOCX/bilingual artifacts, automatic purge, external submission, real infrastructure provisioning and US-09 request-package assembly. US-09 will consume the **exact** effective plan ID and PDF hash from an immutable request snapshot; it must not render the plan again from live data.
