## Concerns (design notes, no defect found)

1. **Any accepted fact of a closed shift marks it late, including a no-op** (the same `box_closed` sent again under another id, an undo that takes nothing, a `box_label`): the plan's rule ("accepted or conflict"). The mark is only a flag for the office; if it should mean "data that changed something", the rule needs per-kind opinion. Not changed.
2. **The station's own close is followed by late data from the station itself** when the station's queue is delivered in more than one batch, or in one batch after the close (pinned by the plan's test): the office will see "late data" on a normally closed shift. Plan-mandated; stage-4 card should show `late_data_at` next to `closed_by_kind` and not read a device-closed shift with late data as an anomaly.
3. **`closedAt` is not bounded** against the shift's `opened_at` or the host clock (a station with a skewed clock closes "before it opened" or in the future; the production record then carries the station's moment). Same family as T4 minor 11 (`closedAt` before `openedAt`): refusing would strand a real close; stage-4 card should show both instants.
4. `close_reason_code` and `late_data_at` have no CHECK in 0009 (it left them open "for plan 2a2"); the six codes and the device/office split of the reason columns are enforced by the handler and the office route only. A CHECK (`close_reason_code IN (...)`, `close_reason_text` NULL when `closed_by_kind = 'device'`) in a later migration would make the production record self-guarding; not added (0010 untouched, not a defect of this task).
5. The lock-wait test (4) uses a 300 ms sleep on the office side; it is one-directional (if the batch were slower to start than the commit the result is the same), so it can only pass vacuously, never fail spuriously. The in-transaction read of the shift is also what X5 and X16 check from the other side.
