## 2. The addendum A–E: all five applied

**A. «Local database», the feed revision larger than a page's 1 MiB. APPLIED, with a different figure than the note suggests.**
Now: «(a template delete that clears thousands of products, or a wizard request near axum's default 2 MiB body limit; the app's own stock templates take about 31 KB) comes as a parameter of up to several MiB and passes whole».
Checked:

- The real stock set is 20 templates (16 box + 2 duplicate + 2 pallet) and measures 31,125 bytes as `{name, purpose, spec}` JSON, the largest layout 2,040 bytes. I measured it with a scratch script under `$TMPDIR` (outside the tree, removed) over `packages/domain/dist`. It can never exceed 1 MiB.
- The route's body limit: the host sets no `DefaultBodyLimit` or `RequestBodyLimit` anywhere (grep over `host/src`, `host/tests`, `proto`), `ApiJson` (`error.rs:159-174`) wraps axum's `Json`, and `axum-core-0.5.6/src/ext_traits/request.rs:319` has `DEFAULT_LIMIT = 2_097_152`. So the figure is 2 MiB, as the reviewer suggested.
- A wizard request near it is a revision over 1 MiB: the wizard writes every template at one revision (`host/tests/bootstrap.rs:964`, `rev = 1`); a feed page ends before a revision past `MAX_PAGE_BYTES` = 1 MiB (`routes/changes.rs:43`) but the first revision comes whole (`changes.rs:158`); each layout is up to 256 KB (`validate.rs:396`), the cap is 32 templates (`bootstrap.rs:32`).
- A wizard at the limit is about 2 MiB, not «several», so I took «several MiB» off the wizard: «a parameter of up to several MiB». `src-tauri/tests/local_db.rs:343-393` (`one_value_of_a_few_mebibytes_passes_the_limits`, a parameter over 3 MiB) still pins the claim.
