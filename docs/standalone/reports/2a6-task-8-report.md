## What was done

- `src/floor/work/WorkScreen.tsx`: the stub is replaced. Scans of the floor's source (`source.start`) run one at a time in a per-screen queue (`createWorkQueue`, kept by `useState`), each handed to `track` in the turn before the queue starts it and touching the idle timer; a scan during `leaving`, or while any stop stands, is refused in words, by flash and by tone and is not written. Closing (`closeBox`) is one function for every path (full box after a scan, entry, confirmation, «Повторить», the block coming back), built from module-level `attemptClose` (+ `settleUnsure`). Entry: totals, then the close of a box left full. Serials: one block supply per screen, half rule on entry (online), after every close and when the link returns; a block that arrives closes a waiting box. Verdicts: line under the box, `SignalOverlay` (title + the words as `detail`), generated tone. Plan notice once. Confirmation of an incomplete close, focus on the safe answer.
- `src/floor/work/signal-sound.ts` (new): port of the cloud station's tones, generated, one `AudioContext` per page.
- `src/floor/work/box-close.ts`: `readClosedBox` (N12), `CLOSED_BOX_SQL`, `BOX_CODES_SQL` (the digest's codes statement now one exported constant used by both the close and the read-back); the doc of `ClosingBox.codeHashes` now says «sorted by hash» (N16 m6, a one-word comment fix I made on the way).
- `src/floor/FloorApp.tsx`, `src/app/App.tsx`: `checkCode`, `requestBlock`, `requestFacts`, `rejectionPending` wired as in the brief; `rejectionPending` is now true also for a held other host.
- `src/app/shell.ts`: items 26 and N3 (see below). `src/floor/FloorStatusBar.tsx`: optional `otherHostHeld` (words of the held other host), `ru.json` keys `floor.work.*` (stub gone) and `floor.status.otherHostHeld`; `shell.css` `.work-screen*`.
- Query-plan lists (item 5): `CLOSED_BOX_SQL` and `BOX_CODES_SQL` in `test/floor-schema.test.ts` (Node) and, by exact text, in `src-tauri/tests/local_db.rs` (`the_floors_repeating_reads_search_an_index`: 29 -> 30 entries); parity test extended (6 -> 8 texts).
