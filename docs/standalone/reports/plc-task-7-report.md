Nothing observable in practice (the label panel takes the screen at once, so the words are shown for a frame at most; the flow-level test renders them), left verbatim as the controller asked.
2. The scan feedback of the choice (tone/flash «КОРОБ ВЫБРАН», «НЕТ ТАКОГО КОРОБА») is the work screen's and still says «короб» for a pallet's choice; the flow's own words (hint, not-found, heading) are the pallet's. A verdict carrying the subject would fix it; not in the brief.
3. A pallet closed whose every box was taken apart stays listable (boxCount 0) — intended (the disassembly spends the SSCC; reprint of its label stays possible).
4. A pallet disassembled while ITS label waits is refused by the screen (labelWaits); the underlying Task 4 behaviour (stale on «Напечатать снова») is therefore unreachable from the screen.
5. Not exercised: Windows, real printers/touch (64 px only checked as the `mk-btn--floor` class), the host, a browser. No Rust changed.
