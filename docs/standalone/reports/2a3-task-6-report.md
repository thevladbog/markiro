## Decision on queued minors item 1 (line breaks in the reason)

The host refuses any control character, including a line break, in a close reason (`validate::is_plain`). `closeReasonRule` already returns `rules.controlCharacter` for it, and the dialog shows it under the field as the refusal text («Уберите табуляцию, перенос строки или другой служебный символ»), with `aria-invalid`, focus on the field and nothing sent. I did NOT block Enter: a key that silently does nothing is worse for keyboard users, and a paste carries the same character anyway. A line break at the very end/start is trimmed and sent without it. Pinned by 4 tests (inside line break, tab, carriage return, unpaired surrogate; each then sent once mended) and a trailing-break case. Items 11-12 (Tab out of the calendar, refused id in the form value) do not apply to these dialogs.
